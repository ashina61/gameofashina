# Master-map building prototypes — 12 October 2026

First camera-matched batch: municipal Divanhane, Kışla and Medrese. These replace the old corner-facing diamond silhouettes only in the unpublished master-map review branch. Current prototypes deliberately have one design each: full level-specific variants and the remaining buildings are not completed in this batch. Game levels, stage growth, saves and construction rules are unchanged.

Camera: elevated, front-facing, nearly horizontal front/rear ground edges. Warm upper-left light; ivory limestone, muted terracotta and lead-blue domes. Kışla is identified by a training court and weapon racks; Medrese by a visible brass telescope and study court. Shared land-plot compatibility is measured in the existing master projection; ground anchors are recorded in `master-building-art.ts`.

Assets: `public/images/game/buildings/master-v2/*-prototype.webp`, with 768px `-sm` variants. Built-in imagegen was used with the user's `1000146426.png` as the camera/style reference. Alpha was checked directly: the image has a real alpha channel and sampled exterior pixels are zero. Colored RGB values outside that zero-alpha silhouette are not an opaque background.

Validation: static production build, TypeScript, ESLint and 347 existing tests passed. Actual Chromium/Phaser renders are saved under `docs/mockups/buildings-master-v2/`; the source alpha and runtime alignment are checked there. No deployment. Previous unchanged HUD sizing/font findings from the full layout audit remain in `MASTER-MAP-ADAPTATION.md`.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent 2D strategy-game municipal building sprite for the attached master city map. Input image is camera, scale, material and lighting reference ONLY; do not redraw its landscape. Create ONE Ottoman–Aegean municipal Divanhane, an elegant medium-sized civic building with a prominent central lead-blue dome, small symmetrical terracotta-roofed side wings, ivory limestone, arched front entrance and restrained brass crescent finial. It must look painted by the same artist as the municipal building in the supplied map, with the same realistic painterly detail, warm upper-left daylight and small soft lower-right shadows. CRITICAL CAMERA: elevated near-frontal orthographic view looking north from the bottom of the map, centered symmetric front facade; roof visible from above; front and rear ground edges run almost horizontally. NOT a 45-degree diamond/corner-facing isometric building. Match the rectangle/trapezoid vacant plots in the reference. One common modular rectangular ground footprint with width:depth approximately 2:1; municipality may be taller than ordinary buildings but cannot require a special surrounding terrain tile. Building occupies a centered footprint with front door pointing downward, same scale of doors/windows as the source. Keep silhouette distinctive as the civic center, not a mosque, no minarets. A small entrance stair may stay inside its own footprint. Actual transparent background with empty transparent pixels around the sprite. No land tile, island, grass slab, fence, trees, roads, plaza, large built-in shadow, writing, UI, border or other buildings. A single fully visible building, no clipping. Export as a standalone transparent sprite, not a contact sheet.
