## 1. MASTER ART ROOT — her üretimde kullan

```text
Original premium Ottoman–Aegean mobile strategy game art for PAYITAHT ADALARI.
One coherent hand-painted asset family, painterly-realistic / realistic-stylized, historically grounded and readable on a phone screen.
World objects use true 2:1 orthographic isometry with no perspective convergence.
Warm late-afternoon daylight from upper-left; soft shadow toward lower-right.
Cream limestone and lime plaster, muted terracotta roof tiles, lead-blue domes, weathered walnut, aged brass, olive/cypress/pine greens and restrained Aegean turquoise.
Thin dark-brown edge definition only where needed; never thick black outlines, flat vector fills, neon fantasy, plastic toy 3D, cartoon exaggeration or generic AI fantasy clutter.
Strong clean silhouettes and believable material texture.
Ottoman / Mediterranean architectural language without copying any existing game composition.
No letters, words, numbers, labels, dates, logos, watermark or baked UI text.
Isolated objects have genuine transparent alpha, clean edges and no background halo.
Keep the complete object inside the canvas with comfortable breathing room.
All assets must feel painted by the same art team.
```

## 3. BUILDING ROOT

```text
True 2:1 orthographic isometric Ottoman–Aegean strategy-game building, transparent background. Common modular land-building ground-contact scale. Do not bake roads, plot-specific paving, grass parcel, city wall, UI, labels or large surrounding scenery into the building. Main permanent architecture only, with a small soft contact shadow. Upper-left warm light. Building silhouette must explain its function at mobile size.
Three evolution stages must represent the same institution: Stage I modest, Stage II established, Stage III prestigious; growth is architectural and functional, not just uniformly scaled up.
```
